# 📊 Sales & Customer Performance Analytics

### 🚀 Project Overview

An interactive Tableau Business Intelligence project analyzing **sales, customer behavior, product performance, and profitability from 2018 to 2022**.

The project features **5 interactive dashboards** designed to turn business data into meaningful insights and support data-driven decision-making.

### 📈 Explore the 5 Dashboards

**1. 📊 Sales Performance Overview**
- Monitor total sales, profit, orders, and profit margin.
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- Compare sales across product categories and countries.
- Filter performance by reporting period.

**2. 👥 Customer Performance Overview**
- Identify the top 10 customers by sales.
- Segment 800 customers into High, Medium, and Low Value groups.
- Explore customer clusters and score distributions.

**3. 💰 Customer Profitability Analysis**
- Compare customer sales against profit.
- Identify profitable and loss-making customers.
- Investigate customer-level profitability patterns.

**4. 🛍️ Product Sales Performance**
- Rank the top-performing products.
- Compare subcategories using an interactive treemap.
- Switch between Sales and Profit using a dynamic parameter.

**5. 📉 Product Profitability & Distribution**
- Identify profitable and unprofitable subcategories.
- Explore geographic sales distribution.
- Analyze sales trends and top products by profit.

### 🛠️ Technical Skills Demonstrated

- 🔗 **Data Modeling:** Relationships between Customers, Orders, and Products.
- 📊 **Data Visualization:** KPI cards, bar charts, treemaps, maps, scatterplots, histograms, and line charts.
- 🧮 **Tableau Calculations:** Calculated fields, aggregations, profit margin, and customer value segmentation.
- 🎛️ **Parameters:** Dynamic switching between Sales and Profit.
- 🔍 **Dashboard Interactivity:** Date filtering and interactive chart selections.
- 👥 **Customer Analytics:** Customer segmentation, clustering, and ranking.
- 🌍 **Business Analytics:** Geographic analysis, time-series trends, and profitability comparisons.

### 💡 Business Value

This project helps users:

- Identify high-value customers and profitable products.
- Discover loss-making customers and subcategories.
- Monitor revenue growth and profit performance.
- Compare business performance across customer and product segments.

### 🖱️ How to Explore

Use the five dashboard tabs to navigate between analytical views. Adjust available filters, switch product metrics between Sales and Profit, and hover over visualizations for additional details.

**🧰 Tools:** Tableau Public | Business Intelligence | Data Analytics
